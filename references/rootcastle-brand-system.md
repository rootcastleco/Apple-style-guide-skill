# Rootcastle Engineering & Innovation Brand System

## Identity

Primary organization name:

**Rootcastle Engineering & Innovation**

Approved short forms:

- **Rootcastle** for public brand references
- **REI** where the product or context already establishes the organization

Primary web identities:

- `rootcastle.com`
- `batuhanayribas.com`

Use “by Rootcastle Engineering & Innovation” or “A Rootcastle product” as restrained ownership lines. Do not repeat the company name across every surface.

## Brand character

- engineering-led
- technically credible
- precise and measured
- durable rather than fashionable
- anti-hype
- occasionally dry or ironic, but never at the expense of clarity or trust

## Color tokens

Core palette:

- `rc-black`: `#000000`
- `rc-blue`: `#0E3D8A`
- `rc-green`: `#228B55`

Recommended semantic mapping:

- `rc-blue`: primary brand accent, selection, navigation emphasis, informational emphasis
- `rc-green`: confirmed healthy state, successful completion, active telemetry when semantically correct
- `rc-black`: foundational dark surface, text, or high-contrast brand field

Rules:

- Do not use brand green as the only success signal.
- Do not force brand colors onto system destructive, warning, or permission semantics.
- Derive accessible light/dark variants and verify contrast in the actual composition.
- Use one dominant brand accent per surface. Blue and green should not compete for primary action status.
- On dense industrial dashboards, reserve saturated colors for actionable or exceptional states.

## Typography

Product interfaces:

- Prefer the platform system typeface for native applications.
- Prefer a highly legible variable sans-serif with strong numeric forms for web and cross-platform products.
- Use tabular numerals for telemetry, measurements, timestamps, and aligned financial data.

Brand and retro accents:

- Determination Sans or Determination Mono may be used only where legally available and already supplied by the project.
- Never bundle or redistribute font files through this skill.
- Limit pixel/retro typography to logos, campaign headers, badges, easter eggs, or low-density decorative regions.
- Do not use pixel fonts for forms, long text, alerts, tables, telemetry, accessibility-critical content, or small labels.

## Visual language

Rootcastle may combine modern platform-native structure with restrained retro/pixel references.

Use retro cues through:

- subtle grid or stepped geometry
- low-frequency pixel motifs
- compact status glyphs
- deliberately limited texture
- monochrome or two-color decorative illustrations

Do not use retro cues through:

- low-resolution controls
- fake terminal interfaces for ordinary tasks
- excessive scan lines, noise, chromatic aberration, or glitch effects
- ornamental borders around every panel
- hidden affordances or intentionally cryptic labels

## Materials and depth

- Use glass, blur, translucency, and depth only to establish hierarchy or preserve context.
- Keep content and controls legible over all materials.
- Avoid stacking multiple translucent surfaces without a clear depth model.
- For industrial and telemetry products, prefer stable surfaces and predictable contrast over decorative transparency.
- Motion should communicate state transition, continuity, or causality. Support reduced motion.

## Product-specific adaptation

### Industrial and telemetry products

Prioritize:

- sunlight and low-light readability
- high information density with strong grouping
- alarm severity and acknowledgement state
- stale-data and connectivity indicators
- unit, timestamp, source, and confidence visibility
- keyboard and large-target operation where field conditions require it
- auditability for commands and remote actions

### Consumer and public products

Prioritize:

- calm onboarding
- progressive disclosure
- concise explanations
- privacy transparency
- polished empty and success states
- familiar platform behavior

### batuhanayribas.com

Use a more personal research-and-engineering voice:

- first-person authorship is acceptable
- show evidence, projects, papers, prototypes, and measured outcomes
- avoid exaggerated founder language and generic “visionary” claims
- connect Rootcastle work through authorship and project provenance, not repeated promotion

## Attribution and legal boundaries

- Do not use Apple trademarks, logos, proprietary product imagery, or SF Symbols outside their applicable license and platform context.
- Do not describe Rootcastle products as Apple products or imply Apple endorsement.
- “Apple-inspired” must mean principle-driven, not a visual clone.
- Preserve Rootcastle ownership in repository documentation and package metadata.
